---
name: Company evidence validity
description: Reusable evidence review and deletion semantics
---

Company evidence must be reviewed separately for each tender. Expired, future-issued or scope-mismatched evidence cannot support current claims, even if previously approved. Missing expiry dates are a visible caution, not a permanent prohibition after human applicability review.

**Why:** Company profiles and project references may have no formal expiry; historical eligibility and tender-specific authorizations must not be assumed current or universal.

**How to apply:** Treat issue/expiry as inclusive calendar dates and compare expiry against both today and tender closing. Scope or metadata edits invalidate prior applicability reviews. Keep historical proposals as style guidance only.

Removing evidence from the reusable library preserves dated metadata snapshots and original files in tenders that still use it.

**Why:** Removing a library entry must not destroy another tender's supporting record.

**How to apply:** Check all library and tender references before physical deletion, including tender snapshots whose library reference has been removed.
