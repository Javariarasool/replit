---
name: Proposal export boundaries
description: Human-reviewed drafting readiness and commercial confidentiality in proposal and checklist exports
---

Drafting readiness means current, human-reviewed inputs with linked supporting material and confirmed supplier/pricing decisions, not independent certification that a bid satisfies the tender.

**Why:** Attaching a document or approving a requirement is a human decision, not proof that every claim is supported. A generated draft must still expose unsupported claims and signature/form actions for final human review.

**How to apply:** Require explicit AI consent before drafting; never automatically certify or approve the output merely because the checklist is ready. Reject drafts that omit assessed requirements rather than silently saving partial coverage.

Technical/commercial separation applies to requirement workbooks as well as proposals. Tender-required financial qualification figures are distinct from quoted supplier or bid prices.

**Why:** A compliance spreadsheet may be included in a technical envelope. Accidentally including unit prices reveals the commercial bid; removing required annual-turnover or past-project values can instead make the technical response incomplete.

**How to apply:** Keep quoted prices in commercial documents or a clearly separated pricing worksheet, and warn that all-group workbooks contain commercial information. Allow source-supported financial eligibility figures when the tender requires them; do not interpret every currency amount as a quoted bid price.
