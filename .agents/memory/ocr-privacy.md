---
name: OCR privacy boundary
description: Why document recognition is local and how consent must remain separate from drafting
---

Document recognition is intentionally local, not a vision-model upload.

**Why:** Tender and company evidence can be sensitive. Local recognition provides OCR without broadening the existing consent to send reviewed text for proposal drafting.

**How to apply:** If a future change introduces a remote OCR or vision provider, require explicit, provider-named consent before transmitting document images. Do not treat draft-generation consent or human OCR verification as permission to send original images.
